# Main-Menu Swordsmen Facing Correction

## Goal

Correct the two swordsmen in `res://pics/main_menu_background_phone.png` so
their poses follow the diagonal between their current slabs and they visibly
face one another.

## Approved Edit

Use pose-only image editing with tight regions around both complete figures,
including their robes and swords:

- the lower-left swordsman faces diagonally up and right;
- the upper-right swordsman faces diagonally down and left; and
- torsos, heads, feet, robes, and sword stances all support those directions,

Their feet remain near the centers of their current slabs. Their current scale,
visual weight, costumes, and restrained ink-painted detail remain consistent
with the source image.

Do not alter the nine arena slabs, seams, railings, towers, ribbons, mountains,
mist, lighting, palette, texture, composition, image dimensions, or empty title
area. Do not add or remove characters, text, weapons, scenery, or decoration.

figures should also be the direction of their confrontation: lower-left toward
upper-right and upper-right toward lower-left. Neither figure should read as
looking purely horizontally across its row.
