Vision

You're creating a wuxia card game that captures the elegant, contemplative beauty of classical ink painting while channeling the kinetic energy of martial arts cinema. This is a game where strategy, storytelling, and Eastern aesthetics converge in a single, focused experience.

Genre and Inspirations

Your core inspiration is the Triple Triad card system from Final Fantasy XIV: A Realm Reborn. That game's genius lies in its elegant rules, quick decision-making, and the satisfying moment when a perfectly-placed card shifts the board state. You're taking that proven, addictive card loop and planting it firmly in a wuxia world, where every match feels like a duel of wit and philosophy.

Look and Feel

Imagine brushstrokes in black ink on rice paper: flowing, minimal, but alive with motion. Your aesthetic pulls from classical East Asian painting while also embracing the vibrant reds, golds, and silks of martial arts cinema. The card designs themselves might echo calligraphy and landscape motifs. The atmosphere is contemplative but never static. There's tension in every match, drama in every dialogue exchange.

Core Mechanics

The player's moment-to-moment experience is this: engage in card battles using the Triple Triad ruleset, where positioning and card synergy matter. Between or around these matches, the player experiences branching conversations and story choices that give context and weight to each encounter. The dialogue trees create a sense of progression and relationship building that makes each card duel feel like part of a larger narrative journey, not just isolated matches.

What Makes It Special

Most card games treat story as window dressing. You're inverting that: the cards are the window, and the dialogue is the heart. By threading narrative and choice throughout a proven, tight card system, you create something that feels both mechanically satisfying and emotionally resonant. The wuxia setting gives you thematic richness for your storytelling and a natural aesthetic identity that's distinctive in the indie space.
